Frequently Asked Questions

Fire Extinguisher & Safety Guide Dhaka

Where to buy certified fire extinguishers in Dhaka, Bangladesh?

N.I. Engineering Services is the leading certified fire extinguisher supplier in Dhaka, offering ABC Dry Powder, CO2, and Foam fire extinguishers located at GA-85(Gr Floor), Middle Badda, Gulshan, Dhaka.

How much does fire extinguisher refilling cost in Dhaka?

Fire extinguisher refilling costs vary by chemical type and capacity (2kg, 6kg, 9kg). N.I. Engineering Services provides factory-certified chemical refilling, pressure testing, and nitrogen purging at reasonable rates in Dhaka.

Which fire safety equipment is required for building compliance in Dhaka?

According to Bangladesh Fire Service and Civil Defence (BFSCD) regulations, commercial and residential buildings require ABC Dry Powder Extinguishers, Hose Reels, Automatic Smoke Detectors, and Fire Hydrant Systems.
